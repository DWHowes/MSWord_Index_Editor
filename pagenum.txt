To investigate

If you change a page style (set to bold for example), Word does not handle that elegantly. Any entry with a non-standard page style should be listed separately in the index. Word will fold that bold or italic page reference into a page range. Even worse, the page range can pick up the page style of decorated reference, if it opens or closes the range.

Is there anything we can do to avoid this, or is it simply a restriction of the word indexing module?

Note that this behaviour is evident in the index for the CUP monograph?
